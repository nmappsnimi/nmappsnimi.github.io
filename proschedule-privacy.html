1. Introduction

Welcome to NM APPS. We provide ProSchedule, an application designed for service owners to seamlessly schedule appointments, manage pricing, and organize client information across mobile and desktop platforms.

We believe that privacy is a fundamental right. Because ProSchedule is designed with a local-first architecture, you retain absolute control over your information. This policy explains how our app interacts with your devices, your personal Google Drive, and how we protect your privacy in compliance with global frameworks, including the EU/UK GDPR, Canada's PIPEDA, and US state privacy laws (like the CCPA).

2. What We Handle & How It Flows

Because of how ProSchedule is built, your business operations remain completely private to you.

A. Your Business Data (Controlled Entirely by You)
This includes your appointment schedules, service prices, notes, and your clients' contact information.

  • Where it lives: This data is stored locally on your devices and, if you choose to enable Pro features, synced directly to your personal Google Drive.
  • Our access: NM APPS cannot access, view, or modify this data. It never touches our servers. You are the sole owner and controller of this information.

B. Subscription & Sync Data (For Pro Users)
To enable Pro features on both your mobile and desktop devices, we store your subscription status and an anonymous unique identifier on a secure cloud database (via Google Firestore).

  • Anonymity: This identifier is a cryptographic "hash" of your Google ID. It does not allow us to see your name or email address.
  • Purpose: This link allows the ProSchedule Desktop app to verify that you have an active subscription on your mobile device without needing a traditional login.

C. Technical Data (Analytics)
To keep ProSchedule stable and secure, we collect basic technical logs (device type, OS version, and anonymous crash reports) via Google Analytics and Firebase.

  • Why we need it: This helps us fix bugs and improve performance. (Legal basis: Legitimate interest in maintaining a functional application).

3. Google Drive Integration

ProSchedule uses your Google account to provide seamless cross-platform sync.

  • Private appDataFolder: The app only accesses a hidden, private folder on your Google Drive created specifically for ProSchedule backups.
  • Desktop Sync: This integration allows you to back up your data on your phone and instantly restore it on your computer.
  • No Credentials Shared: We never receive, view, or transmit your Google password or your personal Google Drive files.

4. Payments and Financial Data

All financial transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Billing Library.

  • We do not collect or store your credit card numbers or billing addresses.
  • Google Play processes your payment and securely updates our verification system to unlock your Pro features on all your connected devices.

5. Your Privacy Rights

Because your data lives on your own devices and Google Drive, you have ultimate control:

  • Access and Portability: You can export your data directly from the app or download your database file from your Google Drive at any time.
  • Right to Unlink: You can sign out of your Google account within the app settings to stop synchronization.
  • Deletion: Uninstalling the app and deleting your backup from Google Drive permanently erases your business data. Because we do not host your data, we cannot recover it for you if it is deleted.

6. Data Security

The security of your data depends on your device security. We highly recommend:

  1. Securing your devices with a passcode or biometric lock.
  2. Enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your Google account to protect your Drive backups.
  3. Using the Pro "App Protection" feature to add an extra layer of security to the app itself.

7. Children's Privacy

ProSchedule is a business tool designed for adults. We do not knowingly track or collect information from children under the age of 18.
