1. Introduction

Welcome to NM APPS. We provide ProSchedule, an application designed for service owners to seamlessly schedule appointments, manage pricing, and organize client information across mobile and desktop platforms.

We believe that privacy is a fundamental right. Because ProSchedule is designed with a local-first architecture, you retain absolute control over your information. This policy explains how our app interacts with your devices, your personal Google Drive, and how we protect your privacy in compliance with global frameworks (GDPR, PIPEDA, CCPA).

2. What We Handle & How It Flows

A. Your Business Data (Controlled Entirely by You)
This includes your appointment schedules, service prices, notes, and your clients' contact information. NM APPS cannot access, view, or modify this data. It never touches our servers.

B. Subscription & Sync Data (For Pro Users)
To enable Pro features on both mobile and desktop, we store your subscription status and an anonymous unique identifier (a cryptographic hash of your Google ID) on a secure cloud database. This does not allow us to see your name or email address.

C. Technical Data (Analytics)
We collect basic technical logs (device type, OS version, and anonymous crash reports) to help us fix bugs and improve performance.

3. Google Drive Integration

ProSchedule uses your Google account for seamless cross-platform sync. The app only accesses a hidden, private folder (appDataFolder) created specifically for your backups. We never receive or view your Google password or your personal Drive files.

4. Payments and Financial Data

Financial transactions are processed securely through the Google Play Billing Library. We do not collect or store your credit card numbers or banking details.

5. Your Privacy Rights

You have ultimate control over your data, including the right to access, portability, and deletion. You can stop synchronization at any time by signing out of your Google account within the app settings. Uninstalling the app and deleting your backup from Google Drive permanently erases your business data.

6. Data Security

We recommend securing your devices with a passcode or biometric lock and enabling Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your Google account to protect your cloud backups.
